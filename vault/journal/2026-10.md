### 2026-10-10

- **M0** Bootstrap: the module `github.com/neuroplastio/myelin` (go
  1.24.0), Apache-2.0, AGENTS.md, this vault, mise (Go 1.26.8), and
  `make check` through `scripts/check.sh`. msgp's generator is pinned in a
  nested tools module. _(Decisions: D1)_
- **M1** Keys: Key, ParseKey, ParseHottyKey, FormatKey, DisplayKey,
  ParseSeq, FormatSeq and TypesText, checked against HOTTY's conformance
  vectors and fuzzed. _(Decisions: D3)_
- **M2** The data types and their msgpack codec, with round-trip tests and
  the timestamp check. _(Decisions: D2, D4)_
- **M7** myelintea, the Bubble Tea adapter, as a module of its own.
  _(Decisions: D5)_
- **SPELLING** One spelling per key, strictly: ParseKey reads only what
  FormatKey writes, and refuses anything else with the spelling to write.
  The maintainer approved it. _(Decisions: D3)_
- **M3–M6** The engine: NewEnv, Compile and Build (layers, drops, the
  leader, unbinds and the shallow-unbind warning); Match, Effective,
  KeysFor and KeyFor, Explain, Shadowed and partial evaluation; Menu and
  Tree; Feed, Expire and Arm.
  - Benchmarks on an AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370: a key nothing starts with is
    36 ns with one allocation; a contextual match 0.53 µs; compiling a
    context about 30 µs; the leader's menu over a hundred bindings 47 µs.

  _(Decisions: D6, D7)_

  resolves the same through myelin: 3,132 lines over 18 surfaces.
- **M8** The reference page, `docs/keymaps.md`, with its named-key table
  generated from the code and checked for drift.
- **CHECKSEQ** CheckSeq, a written sequence checked without a keymap, at
  kubecom's request. kubecom then built green against myelin locally.

  Gate for every leg: a green local `make check`. _(Decisions: D8)_
